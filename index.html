Notice of Restricted Record

This archive contains the regulations governing the formation, recognition and conduct of criminal organisations within Ash Creek. Information concerning gang membership, leadership, territory, affiliations and active criminal activity is withheld from public record. Knowledge contained within this archive must not be treated as information known by any character unless obtained through legitimate roleplay.
